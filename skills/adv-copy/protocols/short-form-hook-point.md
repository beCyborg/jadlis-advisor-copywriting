# Hook Point — Brendan Kane  ·  prefix [HP]  ·  short-form protocol
> In a feed you get ~3 seconds (often 1) to earn the next 30. Use this lens whenever copy lives inside a scroll — Reels/TikTok/Shorts hooks, meme cards, thumbnails, first-line captions, cold-opens — where the job is to stop the thumb before persuading.

## Principles

### [HP:SF01] The 3-second world  (Intro/3)
- **Essence:** A view registers at ~3s but the viewer decides in 1; no hook, no story. Fails for warm/opt-in audiences who already granted attention.
- **Move:** Budget the whole open for one job — buy 3 seconds. Offer/story/CTA are downstream and moot if the open fails.
- **Ex:** EN: "I quit a $200k job for this —" then reveal (not: 4s of logo animation). RU: «Уволился из Яндекса ради этого» → суть.
- **Book:** Kane's premise — platforms count a view at 3s; people scroll hundreds of feet of feed daily.

### [HP:SF02] Hook Point anatomy  (ch 1)
- **Essence:** A hook is built from any of six materials — text, insight/stat/POV, concept/format, personality/performance, or the product itself — whichever grabs fastest. Fails with five mediocre materials and no standout.
- **Move:** Before writing, pick your strongest material; don't default to text if a strange format or the product's oddity is the real stopper.
- **Ex:** EN: re-sing one hit in 10 genres every 10s (not: "hi guys, welcome back"). RU: один хит в стиле 10 исполнителей по 10 сек.
- **Book:** Kane's hook categories; product-as-hook — rope-free El Capitan climb, Tesla's deliberately odd truck.

### [HP:SF03] Hook is not clickbait  (ch 1/3)
- **Essence:** Clickbait grabs with no payoff; a Hook Point ties to real, promised value, and retention now throttles empty hooks. A provocative open is fine only if the body earns it.
- **Move:** After drafting, ask "does the content deliver what this promised?" If not, change the hook or raise the content — one truth for line and story.
- **Ex:** EN: "Meditation is a scam" → honest breakdown (not: "You won't BELIEVE…"). RU: «Медитация — развод» → честный разбор ошибок.
- **Book:** Kane vs empty viral content that got clicks but built no brand; platforms caught on.

### [HP:SF04] Subvert expectations  (ch 2)
- **Essence:** Flip a belief the audience holds as settled; the contradiction is a pattern-interrupt that stops the scroll. Strongest in crowded niches; fails if the flip is unrelated to your message.
- **Move:** Write the cliché your niche repeats, invert it into the hook, use the body to reveal the nuance — without sliding into pure contrarian noise.
- **Ex:** EN: "Warning: safety is dangerous" (not: "5 tips for a productive morning"). RU: «Дисциплина переоценена» → решают системы.
- **Book:** Kane's "Safety is Dangerous"; Gary Vee's "you're gonna die" answer to "three inspiring words?"

### [HP:SF05] Specific bold claim  (ch 2)
- **Essence:** A concrete, specific, slightly audacious claim beats a general one — specificity feels real and testable. Fails when the number is inflated past belief.
- **Move:** Swap "how to grow" for a numbered, time-boxed, image-forming claim: the number, the timeframe, the exact outcome.
- **Ex:** EN: "Zero to a million followers in 30 days" (not: "Improve your marketing"). RU: «0 → 100 000 подписчиков в Telegram за 21 день».
- **Book:** Ferriss A/B-tested "The 4-Hour Workweek" against weaker titles in AdWords — the specific one won.

### [HP:SF06] Enter the pain conversation  (ch 2)
- **Essence:** Join the conversation already in the prospect's head; describe their problem better than they can and they assume you hold the solution. Fails on a marketer's abstraction of the pain.
- **Move:** Open with the exact symptom in their words, before mentioning your thing. Diagnosis first, offer later.
- **Ex:** EN: "You reread the same paragraph four times and still can't focus." RU: «Открываешь ноут работать — и через минуту листаешь ленту.»
- **Book:** Kane invokes Schwartz's "enter the conversation in their mind"; describing the problem best implies owning the fix.

### [HP:SF07] If/Then formula  (ch 2)
- **Essence:** A reliable starter: IF [specific desire or pain] THEN [payoff / next step] — forces an audience-framed, outcome-led hook. Fails as your only pattern; verbatim at scale it turns formulaic.
- **Move:** Fill both slots concretely, then A/B it against a more off-the-wall hook — the formula is a floor, not the ceiling.
- **Ex:** EN: "If you want first dates with people who have it together, then read this." RU: «Хочешь скинуть 5 кг без спортзала к лету — вот с чего начать.»
- **Book:** Clemens's if/then starter — problem after "if," product after "then" — dating and golf examples.

### [HP:SF08] Curiosity gap: familiar × novel  (ch 2)
- **Essence:** The strongest hooks pair a familiar element with one novel twist, leaving an open loop. Pure novelty confuses; pure familiarity bores.
- **Move:** Anchor on something known, bolt on the one detail that shouldn't be there. The gap between them is the curiosity.
- **Ex:** EN: "Why are there 96 million black balls on this reservoir?" RU: «Зачем бабушка складывает футболки в 3 движения за секунду?»
- **Book:** Kane's "96 million black balls" reframe; an Away suitcase that charges your phone (familiar + novel feature).

### [HP:SF09] Audience-first, not self  (ch 2)
- **Essence:** The commonest failure: writing for what makes you look good, not what the viewer wants — "buy tickets" repels, the experience attracts. Subtle in expert niches.
- **Move:** Delete every hook framed "I want you to X." Reframe from the viewer's outcome. If a hook won't work, check this first.
- **Ex:** EN: "How to fold a fitted sheet in 5 seconds" (not: "Check out our premium linen"). RU: «Как отстирать засохшее пятно за 2 минуты.»
- **Book:** Kane's film case — "buy tickets" ads fall flat; audiences go for the story, not a studio's order.

### [HP:SF10] Give the gag away  (ch 3)
- **Essence:** Linear storytelling dies in the feed. Put the punchline/reveal/best moment in the first ~10s instead of building to it. Fails for long-form where a seated audience tolerates a build.
- **Move:** Find the single most interesting/emotional beat and move it to the front; rebuild tension after you've earned the view.
- **Ex:** EN: transformation clip opens on the final result, then "here's how" (not: 30s of setup). RU: сначала результат, потом «а вот как».
- **Book:** Brownstein (Shareability) — give the punchline/emotional reveal in a social video's first ten seconds.

### [HP:SF11] The 3-second promise  (ch 3)
- **Essence:** The first 3s can't summarize your message; they promise HOW value arrives — clear, well-paced, satisfying. A slick open before a messy body spends trust it can't repay.
- **Move:** Treat the open as a job application for the viewer's time — signal delivery competence, not the full thesis.
- **Ex:** EN: clean static shot + legible meme card readable before the voiceover (not: shaky fast-cut chaos). RU: спокойный кадр + читаемая мем-карта.
- **Book:** Gowda — the first three seconds set delivery expectations and earn viewer/algorithm trust.

### [HP:SF12] The effect is the hero  (ch 3)
- **Essence:** Design for the gut reaction — "so smart," "I relate," "so satisfying" — not for logical analysis. Fails when the piece chases three reactions and lands none.
- **Move:** Decide the one reaction first; make every choice (words, visuals, pace, music) serve it. Reverse-engineer copy from emotion.
- **Ex:** EN: oddly-satisfying pour-and-fold built purely for "ahh" (not: a feature list read aloud). RU: гипнотичная нарезка «залил-сложил» ради «как приятно».
- **Book:** Kane's Gucci/In-N-Out — the hook is the engineered feeling; $20 of product delivers $450 of value.

### [HP:SF13] Frictionless pacing  (ch 3)
- **Essence:** Don't make the viewer think or catch up. Deliver linearly — "first this, then this" — quick but followable. Three ideas in three seconds overloads; that disengages as fast as boredom.
- **Move:** Intro = static scene, one element in, one thing happens, then start. Pair complex text with simple visuals; never load both.
- **Ex:** EN: red cup appears → crushed → set down → tutorial begins (not: five ingredients at once). RU: стакан → сминают → ставят → рецепт.
- **Book:** Gowda's red-cup intro; structure all videos linearly so the viewer processes each beat.

### [HP:SF14] Meme card headline  (ch 3)
- **Essence:** A meme card — burned-in text at top/bottom of a clip — is the native visible hook on IG/FB and sets the delivery promise. Fails with vague filler that tells viewers they must work for you.
- **Move:** Put a value-carrying, complete-thought phrase on the card — not "watch till the end." Bold the key phrase, break the line at the meaning, keep visuals calm while it shows.
- **Ex:** EN: "**Laugh at yourself** — or other people will" (not: "watch till the end"). RU: «**Смейся над собой** — иначе это сделают другие.»
- **Book:** Kane/Gowda on meme cards; Mindvalley tripled views by front-loading Hook Points as meme cards.

### [HP:SF15] Title + thumbnail are the hook  (ch 3)
- **Essence:** On YouTube the packaging — title and thumbnail — decides CTR; past ~10-20% CTR, views jump non-linearly. Retention still gates payoff ([HP:SF03]): a killer package on weak content just fails faster.
- **Move:** Treat title+thumbnail as the deliverable. Mock several into a real suggested-videos screenshot, pick the one that out-competes neighbors, test variants live.
- **Ex:** EN: "Why are there 96 million black balls on this reservoir?" + one absurd thumbnail (not: "Reservoir vlog #4"). RU: «Почему 96 млн чёрных шаров?» + абсурдный тамбнейл.
- **Book:** Muller (Veritasium)/MrBeast reworking a title/thumbnail; you exist only if viewers see them.

### [HP:SF16] Test, don't guess  (ch 2/3)
- **Essence:** Strong hooks come from volume and testing, not inspiration; even top copywriters produce mostly duds and the home runs are unpredictable. Not an excuse to ship untested; beware paid-spiked "winners."
- **Move:** Run the 5-step loop — study winners, dissect failures, generate 50-100 variants, narrow to three, A/B test, repeat.
- **Ex:** EN: launch a Reel with 3 first-lines to small audiences, scale the winner (not: bet on your first clever line). RU: 3 первые строки на малые аудитории → масштаб победителя.
- **Book:** Kane tested 75,000+ variations with Couric, 30+ hooks per interview; Clemens/Ferriss credit A/B testing.

### First-3-seconds build (video)
| Beat | Do | Avoid |
|------|-----|-------|
| Second 0 | one legible hook (meme card / spoken line) | logo animation, slow fade-in |
| Seconds 1-3 | static-then-one-action; readable text; calm visuals | 3 ideas at once, catch-up overload |
| Promise | signal clear/satisfying delivery | summarize the whole thesis |
| Payoff owed | content cashes the hook's check | clickbait with no resolution |

### Short-form copy checklist (before publish)
- [ ] Does the open earn the first 3 seconds on its own? [HP:SF01]
- [ ] Which of the 6 materials is the actual hook here? [HP:SF02]
- [ ] Does the body deliver what the hook promised? [HP:SF03]
- [ ] Specific — number/timeframe/outcome, not vague? [HP:SF05]
- [ ] Framed as the viewer's win, never "buy my thing"? [HP:SF09]
- [ ] Best/most emotional beat front-loaded? [HP:SF10]
- [ ] Meme card / title gives value, not "watch till the end"? [HP:SF14]
- [ ] Graspable in ≤3s without hard thinking? [HP:SF13]
- [ ] ≥3 hook variants queued to test? [HP:SF16]

## Operational notes
- **Hooks decay — find the new next.** A working hook gets copied and saturated within months (Toms' "one for one"). Rotate hooks; keep the brand core fixed.
- **Distribution multiplies the hook.** Same audience, different hook = wildly different yield: on a large aggregator account a weak hook nets ~200 follows, a strong one 5,000-20,000 in a day.
- **Mine adjacent platforms.** Best raw ideas surface first on Reddit and under-packaged low-view YouTube clips — repackage the idea, not the polish; low production value can read as authentic.
- **RU transfer.** Platform-agnostic: meme card ≈ burned-in подпись on Reels/Shorts; the 3-second promise and specificity rules hold across TikTok/Telegram.
