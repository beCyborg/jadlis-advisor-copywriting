# The Adweek Copywriting Handbook — Reusable Copy Patterns  ·  prefix [ADW]
> The concrete, templated moves you can lift straight into a draft — the "how it actually reads on the page" layer beneath the principles ([ADW:01]–[ADW:18]) and triggers ([ADW:T01]–[ADW:T31]). Every example below is INVENTED to illustrate the mechanic; none is copied from the book. RU examples transfer the mechanic to a Russian-market case, not translate the EN one.

### [ADW:P01] The Ultra-Short Opener
- **Maps to:** [ADW:04] first-sentence axiom.
- **Skeleton:** Open with a 3–5 word sentence that carries no selling load — just enough to be read without effort. Follow with slightly longer lines that build the slide.
- **Few-shot (EN):** "It failed twice." / "Your dashboard lies." / "Nobody warned me." → then the story. Negative: "In today's fast-paced competitive landscape, businesses must..." (a wall, not a door).
- **Few-shot (RU):** "Бюджет слили." / "Отчёт врёт." / "Меня кинули." → и дальше история. Антипример: "В условиях высокой конкуренции современный бизнес обязан..." (стена вместо входа).
- **Book ref:** JS&A openers like "It's easy." and "It had to happen." — so short they barely qualify as sentences.

### [ADW:P02] The Offbeat-Story / News Lead
- **Maps to:** [ADW:SS] slide-start, [ADW:T26] storytelling.
- **Skeleton:** Open with a genuinely interesting (often odd) true story or clipped news item → ride reader curiosity → build a bridge sentence that links it to your product (the link can be a tight fit or a deliberate stretch you acknowledge). Keep a swipe file of odd articles.
- **Few-shot (EN):** Lead with a real item about a town that banned left turns to save fuel → "You're wasting the same 8% every day — not on the road, but in your ad account. Here's the detour." Negative: opening cold with "Our software optimizes ad spend."
- **Few-shot (RU):** Начать с реальной новости "деревня запретила левые повороты ради экономии топлива" → "Ты теряешь те же 8% каждый день — не на дороге, а в рекламном кабинете. Вот объезд." Антипример: сразу "Наш сервис оптимизирует расходы на рекламу".
- **Book ref:** He starts a rower ad with a true embezzlement story (then confesses he invented the buyer), and shows how a Forbes item on British underwear habits could open an ad for a washing product — or even a computer.

### [ADW:P03] Seed-of-Curiosity Connectors
- **Maps to:** [ADW:08].
- **Skeleton:** At the end of any paragraph that slows down or transitions, append a tiny forward hook: "But there's more." / "Let me explain." / "Here's the part nobody tells you." Also plant one big open loop near the top that only closes at the offer. Use sparingly.
- **Few-shot (EN):** "...which is exactly when the churn stopped. And the reason surprised even me." Negative: ending six paragraphs in a row with "But wait — there's more!" until it's wallpaper.
- **Few-shot (RU):** "...и именно тогда отписки прекратились. Причина удивила даже меня." Антипример: шесть абзацев подряд заканчивать на "Но это ещё не всё!" — крючок выгорает.
- **Book ref:** He shunts the end of the "Ginger" story into an appendix specifically to keep the reader moving down the slide.

### [ADW:P04] The Anti-Pitch Open
- **Maps to:** [ADW:T02] honesty, [ADW:SS].
- **Skeleton:** Open by attacking or belittling your own product ("ugly name, cheap case, no display — we almost passed") → the reader, expecting a pitch, is disarmed and slides in to find the catch → reverse into the genuine virtues you discovered → close warm. Works because the honesty is unexpected.
- **Few-shot (EN):** "Honestly? The interface looks dated and the onboarding annoyed me. I nearly deleted it. Then week two happened." Negative: "The most revolutionary, beautiful, powerful tool ever built" — the reader's guard goes up, not down.
- **Few-shot (RU):** "Честно? Интерфейс страшный, а онбординг меня взбесил. Чуть не удалил. А потом пошла вторая неделя." Антипример: "Самый революционный и мощный инструмент на рынке" — читатель закрывается, а не открывается.
- **Book ref:** "Magic Baloney" opens by trashing the Magic Stat thermostat — ugly case, stupid name, no readout — then earns its way to praise; ran profitably for three years.

### [ADW:P05] Involvement / Future-Pacing
- **Maps to:** [ADW:T01].
- **Skeleton:** Put the reader inside the experience with second-person sensory commands — "hold it," "feel," "watch," "imagine you already own it." Optionally add a literal involvement device (interactive step) that pulls response up.
- **Few-shot (EN):** "Open the app tomorrow morning. See your real profit per product, already sorted. Feel the Excel night shifts end." Negative: "The dashboard displays per-SKU profitability metrics." (describes; doesn't immerse).
- **Few-shot (RU):** "Открой приложение завтра утром. Видишь реальную прибыль по каждому товару, уже отсортированную. Чувствуешь — ночные смены в Excel закончились." Антипример: "Дашборд отображает метрики рентабельности по SKU" (описывает, но не погружает).
- **Book ref:** He writes the Corvette test-drive in the reader's own senses — wind, the punch of acceleration, heads turning — then adds the "circle the misspelled words for $2 off" device that made him *more* money because buyers found only half.

### [ADW:P06] The Satisfaction Conviction Close
- **Maps to:** [ADW:T09].
- **Skeleton:** At the close, go conspicuously beyond a normal guarantee so the reader thinks "they must really believe in this" or "won't they get ripped off?" Ideally raise the exact objection first, then over-resolve it. It must make sense for the offer.
- **Few-shot (EN):** "If you don't land one new client in 90 days, I'll refund you — and pay for the ad budget you spent testing it." Negative: a bare "30-day money-back guarantee" line that reads like everyone else's.
- **Few-shot (RU):** "Если за 90 дней не придёт ни один клиент — верну деньги и компенсирую бюджет, который ты потратил на тест." Антипример: сухое "гарантия возврата 30 дней", как у всех.
- **Book ref:** His Consumers Hero test: swapping a plain trial-period line for "send back your card and we'll refund your $5 *plus interest*" **doubled** response — even at the very end of the ad.

### [ADW:P07] Raise-and-Resolve Objection Block
- **Maps to:** [ADW:14] sequence, [ADW:T04] credibility.
- **Skeleton:** In the copy's logical flow, voice the reader's next doubt in their own words, then answer it fully and honestly — especially for anything obvious (assembly required, high price, "does it really work?"). Unraised objections quietly kill the sale.
- **Few-shot (EN):** "'But I'm not technical.' Neither was our first 500 users. Setup is three clicks; we'll do it on a call if you'd rather watch." Negative: hiding the "requires integration" fact below the buy button so the doubt festers.
- **Few-shot (RU):** "«Я не технарь.» Как и первые 500 наших пользователей. Настройка — три клика, а хочешь — сделаем на созвоне, ты просто смотришь." Антипример: спрятать "нужна интеграция с 1С" под кнопкой — сомнение остаётся неснятым.
- **Book ref:** In the Fireball pinball ad each copy block answers the exact next question — justify it, will it last, what about service — in the order a live buyer would ask.

### [ADW:P08] The Linking / Naming Bridge
- **Maps to:** [ADW:T14].
- **Skeleton:** Name or frame the unfamiliar product as a familiar concept so the mind does less work: pick a familiar object/idea + attach it to the product → the name itself carries the concept. Great for new, technical, or "miracle" products.
- **Few-shot (EN):** An AI meeting-notes tool named/pitched as "a stenographer who never sleeps"; a fuel additive as "a gym membership for your engine." Negative: "an NLP-based automated transcription and summarization pipeline."
- **Few-shot (RU):** ИИ-расшифровщик встреч подаётся как "стенографистка, которая не спит"; присадка в бак — "абонемент в спортзал для двигателя". Антипример: "конвейер автоматической транскрибации на базе NLP".
- **Book ref:** "The Nose" (a smoke detector that sniffs the air), "Pocket CB," and "vitamins for your car" / "tune-up in a pill" for a hard-to-believe fuel additive.

### [ADW:P09] Specificity Swap
- **Maps to:** [ADW:T29].
- **Skeleton:** Find every vague/puffed claim and replace it with an exact figure or concrete fact. Precision reads as truth and as expertise.
- **Few-shot (EN):** "Loved by dentists" → "92% of dentists surveyed recommend it." "Fast setup" → "Live in 4 minutes 30 seconds — we timed it." Negative: leaving "trusted by thousands" and "lightning fast" as-is.
- **Few-shot (RU):** "Нравится стоматологам" → "92% опрошенных стоматологов рекомендуют". "Быстрая настройка" → "Запуск за 4 минуты 30 секунд — засекали". Антипример: оставить "нам доверяют тысячи" и "молниеносно".
- **Book ref:** "New dentists everywhere use it" (puffery, discounted) vs. "92% of new dentists use and recommend it" (believable); "72,000 nerve endings," "242 miles of blood vessels."

### [ADW:P10] Withhold-the-Conclusion
- **Maps to:** [ADW:T27] mental engagement.
- **Skeleton:** Lay out the facts so the damning/flattering conclusion is inescapable — but *don't state it*. Let the reader's own mind close the loop (whole-brain effort = pleasure + conviction they reached themselves). Vague/implicit beats over-explicit.
- **Few-shot (EN):** "The agency charges $8,000/month and assigns one junior to five clients. We're two seniors, on your account only, for $3,000." (You never say "they're overcharging you.") Negative: "...which means they're ripping you off and we're the smart choice." (spelled out, deflating).
- **Few-shot (RU):** "Агентство берёт 400к в месяц и сажает одного джуна на пять клиентов. Нас двое сеньоров, только на твоём проекте, за 150к." (Не говоришь "они тебя обдирают".) Антипример: "...то есть они тебя грабят, а мы — умный выбор" (разжёвано, слабее).
- **Book ref:** His Seiko comparison lets the reader conclude on their own that jewelers make a fortune per watch — he never says it, and it lands harder for that.

### [ADW:P11] Price-as-Concept / Value Comparison
- **Maps to:** [ADW:10] concept, [ADW:T05] value.
- **Skeleton:** Either (a) reframe the product's whole concept by moving the price (serious tool vs. toy), or (b) prove value by comparing side-by-side to what the buyer already pays for lesser things, or by breaking price into a per-day/per-use figure.
- **Few-shot (EN):** "$99/month sounds like software. It's less than one hour of the freelancer it replaces — every week." Negative: stating "$99/month" with no anchor, leaving the reader to judge it cold.
- **Few-shot (RU):** "3 000₽ в месяц звучит как ещё одна подписка. Это дешевле одного часа фрилансера, которого он заменяет — каждую неделю." Антипример: просто "3 000₽ в месяц" без якоря, читатель оценивает вслепую.
- **Book ref:** The Pocket CB *became a different product* at each price — serious radio at $39.95, walkie-talkie at $29.95, toy at $19.95 — with essentially the same copy.

### [ADW:P12] The Urgency Close
- **Maps to:** [ADW:T20] urgency, [ADW:T21] fear.
- **Skeleton:** End with a true reason to act now — limited edition, price rise, closeout, order-by-date shipping, bonus-for-speed — stated without violating the integrity you built. Also: make sure no missing info gives the reader an excuse to "call later" (i.e., never).
- **Few-shot (EN):** "The 40 founding seats lock in this price for life; after that it's list price. 11 are left as I write this." Negative: "Buy in the next 10 minutes or it's gone forever!!!" on an evergreen product — the false deadline detonates trust.
- **Few-shot (RU):** "40 мест по цене основателей фиксируют тариф навсегда; дальше — прайс. Пока пишу — осталось 11." Антипример: "Купи за 10 минут или потеряешь навсегда!!!" на вечнозелёном продукте — фальшивый дедлайн взрывает доверие.
- **Book ref:** His small retraction ad — "we priced it wrong, price goes up $20, you have a few days at the old price" — created real, honest urgency and outpulled the original larger ad.

### [ADW:P13] The "True Story" Byline Open
- **Maps to:** [ADW:13] personal communication, [ADW:T26] storytelling.
- **Skeleton:** Sign the copy with a real byline and open in first person with a credibility gambit — "I'm about to tell you a true story. If you believe me, you'll be rewarded. If you don't, I'll make it worth your while to change your mind." Then tell the discovery story person-to-person.
- **Few-shot (EN):** "By Anna R. — I almost didn't write this. What happened to my open rate sounds made up. Judge for yourself." Negative: an unsigned "Introducing the all-new..." corporate voice with no human behind it.
- **Few-shot (RU):** "Автор — Анна Р. — Я почти не стала это писать. То, что случилось с моей открываемостью, звучит выдуманно. Суди сам." Антипример: безымянное "Представляем совершенно новый..." — корпоративный голос без человека.
- **Book ref:** "Vision Breakthrough," bylined by Joseph Sugarman, opens "I am about to tell you a true story..." and launched a company that sold ~20 million pairs of BluBlocker sunglasses.
