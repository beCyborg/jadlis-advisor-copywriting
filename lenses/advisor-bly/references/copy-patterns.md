## PRINT & DIRECT MAIL

### [CPH:P1] The eight headline types  (ch 2)
A swipe library: eight time-tested headline structures. Pick by what you have (news? a benefit? a customer quote?) and the audience. Maps to [CPH:03], [CPH:04]. Avoid "blind" headlines that mean nothing without the body copy.

| Type | Mechanic | Invented EN example |
|------|----------|---------------------|
| **1. Direct** | States the proposition plainly, no wordplay | "Ergonomic Office Chairs — 40% Off This Week" |
| **2. Indirect** | Arouses curiosity via double meaning; body copy pays it off | "Ten Reasons Your Best Engineer Is About to Quit" |
| **3. News** | Announces something genuinely new | "Introducing the First CRM That Fills Itself In" |
| **4. How-to** | Promises information/solution ("can't write a bad how-to headline") | "How to Cut Your Cloud Bill 40% Without Firing Anyone" |
| **5. Question** | Asks something the reader wants answered (their self-interest, not yours) | "Is Your Pricing Quietly Capping Your Growth?" |
| **6. Command** | Opens with a strong action verb | "Stop Overpaying Your Payment Processor" |
| **7. Reason-why** | "N reasons / ways / steps" introducing a listed body | "7 Signs You've Outgrown Your Accounting Software" |
| **8. Testimonial** | Customer's own words sell (in quotes, natural tone) | "'We recovered the subscription fee in the first week.'" |

- **Few-shot (RU, how-to):** «Как сократить расходы на облако на 40%, не увольняя ни одного человека».
- **Few-shot (RU, question):** «Не ваши ли тарифы тихо тормозят рост выручки?»
- **Negative (both):** self-serving question "Знаете, чем сейчас занимается наша компания?" — reader's answer is "нет и всё равно", and turns the page.

### [CPH:P2] Quotation-mark / testimonial hook  (ch 2)
Wrapping a headline in quotation marks catches the eye and signals "this is factual / someone said this," lifting believability — even when the line isn't attributed (the "unattributed testimonial"). For real testimonials, use the customer's own unpolished words; a natural, conversational tone reads as true. Maps to [CPH:11].

- **Few-shot (EN):** Real — "'I cancelled two other tools the day we switched.' — Dana R., ops lead." Unattributed — "'The onboarding took nine minutes, not nine days.'"
- **Few-shot (RU):** Реальный — «"Мы окупили годовую подписку за первую неделю". — Марина, руководитель отдела продаж». Безымянный — «"Настройка заняла девять минут, а не девять дней"».
- **Boundary:** don't fabricate attributed quotes (compliance/legal risk, [CPH:02]); the unattributed form works precisely because it claims no speaker.

### [CPH:P3] Blind vs teaser envelope  (ch 7)
The outer envelope is where selling starts. Two approaches:
- **Teaser** — headline/copy on the envelope. Use *only* if the message is irresistible; a weak teaser labels the piece "advertising → trash" and can cut response below a plain envelope. Best teaser form promises a reward inside ("Enclosed is your free ___"), not a boast ("The best ___ ever made").
- **Blind (no teaser)** — plain, personal-looking envelope so the reader opens it to find out what it is. Once opened with a strong lead, the battle is half won. Keep it plain: no bright inserts showing through, no logo, plain return address.

- **Few-shot (EN, reward teaser):** "Inside: the 15-minute setup that saved our readers $8,300 last tax year."
- **Few-shot (RU, reward teaser):** «Внутри: 15-минутная настройка, которая сэкономила нашим читателям 8 300 ₽ на налогах в прошлом году». Слабый (антипример): «Лучшая страховка на рынке» — чистое хвастовство, летит в корзину.

### [CPH:P4] Letter-sells / brochure-tells split  (ch 7–8)
In a multi-piece package, divide labor: the **letter sells** (benefits, emotion, the pitch, the ask); the **brochure/flyer tells** (features, specs, colorful product shots, detailed tables of contents, demonstration photos). Every package needs a letter; a flyer is optional — add one for visually impressive products, demos-by-photo, or detail too heavy for the letter. Maps to [CPH:06], [CPH:13].

- **Few-shot (EN):** Letter: "You'll close your books in an afternoon, not a weekend." Flyer: a feature grid — supported integrations, export formats, security certifications.
- **Few-shot (RU):** Письмо: «Вы будете закрывать месяц за вечер, а не за все выходные». Буклет: таблица характеристик — поддерживаемые интеграции, форматы выгрузки, сертификаты безопасности.

## WEB

### [CPH:P5] Feature-dash-benefit bullet  (ch 12)
The workhorse web bullet: state the feature, dash, then the benefit it delivers. Scannable, and it forces every feature to earn its place with a payoff. Stack many on a sales page; use a shorter set to describe a free download's contents. Maps to [CPH:06].

- **Few-shot (EN):** "Offline mode — keep taking orders even when the venue's Wi-Fi drops." / "One-click export — hand your accountant a clean file, not a weekend of cleanup."
- **Few-shot (RU):** «Офлайн-режим — принимаете заказы, даже когда падает Wi-Fi на площадке». / «Экспорт в один клик — отдаёте бухгалтеру готовый файл, а не выходные разбора».
- **Negative:** a bare feature bullet — "Offline mode" / «Офлайн-режим» — leaves the reader to guess why it matters.

### [CPH:P6] No-navigation, credibility-first page  (ch 12)
Two structural rules that lift conversion: (1) **strip navigation** — a landing page offers one decision, respond or leave; no menu of tempting links to wander off into. (2) **Build credibility on the first screen** — logo/seal, one to three strong testimonials, or a credentials pre-head *above* the headline, because online skepticism is high. Pair with risk reversal (guarantee / "free, no obligation") near the CTA. Maps to [CPH:11], [CPH:12].

- **Few-shot (EN):** Above the fold — company logo, "Trusted by 4,200 clinics," and "'Cut our no-shows 30% in a month.'" then the headline; page has zero nav links, one CTA button.
- **Few-shot (RU):** Первый экран — логотип, «Нам доверяют 4 200 клиник» и «"Сократили неявки на 30% за месяц"», затем заголовок; на странице ноль пунктов меню, одна кнопка CTA.

## EMAIL

### [CPH:P7] The ultra-specific "fascination" bullet  (ch 2, 13)
A curiosity bullet that teases a specific payoff without revealing it — the reader must open/buy/read on to get it. Power comes from ultra-specificity ([CPH:04]) plus an open loop. Bottom Line/Boardroom built an empire on these. Use in emails, bullet blocks, and teasers.

- **Few-shot (EN):** "The one line in your Stripe settings quietly leaking 3% of every payment." / "Why the 'best' time to email your list is almost never when you think."
- **Few-shot (RU):** «Одна строка в настройках эквайринга, из-за которой утекает 3% с каждого платежа». / «Почему "лучшее" время для рассылки почти никогда не совпадает с тем, что вы думаете».
- **Boundary:** the payoff must be real; a fascination that leads to nothing burns trust for the whole list.

### [CPH:P8] FROM/SUBJECT teaser + first-para mini-message  (ch 13)
The open-rate pattern: a **FROM** line chosen for the relationship (List Owner / Your Company / You / "The ___ Team") plus a **SUBJECT** line built like an envelope teaser — curiosity, not blatant promo. Then the **first paragraph delivers the whole message in miniature** with an immediate response link, so a skimmer who reads only line one still gets the offer. Maps to [CPH:03], [CPH:04].

- **Few-shot (EN):** *From:* "The Ledgerly Team" — *Subject:* "The month-end mistake costing you a weekend" — *Para 1:* "Closing your books shouldn't eat your Saturday. Ledgerly does it in an afternoon — see how in 2 minutes: [link]"
- **Few-shot (RU):** *От:* «Команда Ledgerly» — *Тема:* «Ошибка при закрытии месяца, которая крадёт ваши выходные» — *Абзац 1:* «Закрытие книг не должно съедать субботу. Ledgerly делает это за вечер — посмотрите за 2 минуты: [ссылка]»
- **Constraint:** repeat offer + link at the close, ≤3 links total, ~55–60 chars/line, tone informative not hard-sell.

## VIDEO / TV / RADIO

### [CPH:P9] The commercial-type menu (12 formats)  (ch 10)
A menu of proven commercial structures — choose by product and budget. Reason-why and emotion map to [CPH:11] and [CPH:09]/[CPH:10]; demonstration is the strongest proof format when the product can be shown working.

| Format | Core move |
|--------|-----------|
| 1. Demonstration | Show the product working / solving the problem on screen |
| 2. Testimonial | Real customers do the selling ([CPH:11]) |
| 3. Stand-up presenter | A spokesperson delivers the pitch to camera |
| 4. Slice of life | A little dramatized scene the viewer recognizes |
| 5. Lifestyle | Sells the identity/life around the product |
| 6. Animation | Illustrated/animated storytelling |
| 7. Jingle | Music carries the message and memory |
| 8. Visual as hero | A striking image is the whole idea |
| 9. Humor | Comedy carries attention (only if it serves the sell) |
| 10. Continuing characters | A recurring mascot/character builds familiarity |
| 11. Reason-why | Lays out the logical case, point by point |
| 12. Emotion | Leads with feeling — fear, love, pride, nostalgia |

- **Few-shot (EN, demonstration):** a knife-sharpener ad slices a tomato paper-thin right after sharpening a rusty blade on camera — proof beats adjectives.
- **Few-shot (RU, emotion):** ролик каршеринга — отец успевает на утренник дочери, потому что машина оказалась в двух минутах ходьбы; продаётся не тариф, а «не пропустить важное».

### [CPH:P10] Short-video arc + pattern-interrupt / whiteboard VSL  (ch 16)
Two reusable video patterns:
- **Short marketing video (≈1–2 min, 100–120 words/min):** Introduce → **show the problem** → **show the solution** → **show the better outcome** → **CTA**. Use "you," short words, no fluff; every word counts ([CPH:05], [CPH:12]).
- **Long VSL:** open with a **pattern-interrupt** (a line that breaks the viewer's autopilot), then run the "velvet slide" story; state the problem in the lead and reveal the solution within 1–2 minutes. The classic **whiteboard** form shows one or two sentences per slide as a voice-over reads them — keep sentences and paragraphs short since it can't be re-skimmed ([CPH:F9]).

- **Few-shot (EN, pattern-interrupt open):** "If someone told you slow sales are a traffic problem — they were wrong, and it's costing you every day."
- **Few-shot (RU, short-video arc):** «Тратите 15 минут, чтобы собрать данные по одному клиенту? (проблема) Наша платформа собирает всё в одном окне за секунды (решение) — и вы освобождаете час в день на реальную работу (результат). Оставьте заявку на демо прямо сейчас (CTA)».
- **Boundary:** VSL info density ~20% below a text page; ≤2 numbers per sentence, round one; tone positive but authoritative.
