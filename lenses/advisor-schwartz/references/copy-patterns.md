# Breakthrough Advertising — Copy Patterns  ·  prefix [BTA]
> A working headline/lead library keyed to the two diagnostic axes: **awareness** ([BTA:16]) decides *what the headline says*, **sophistication** ([BTA:17]) decides *what device makes the claim land*. All examples are invented and set on modern products — the book's 1966 cases are dead; transfer the mechanics, not the words.

## Principle index (this file)
| Code | Principle | Ch | Essence (≤12 words) |
|------|-----------|----|---------------------|
| [BTA:18] | Awareness × Sophistication Pattern Matrix | 2–3 | Pick the headline skeleton from the market's two coordinates. |

## How to use
1. Locate the market on **awareness** (states 1–5) and **sophistication** (stages 1–5) first — see `awareness-sophistication.md`.
2. **Awareness sets the row** (opening content: offer / product+superiority / crystallized desire / named problem / identity lead).
3. **Sophistication sets the device** (bare claim → bigger claim → new mechanism → bigger mechanism → identification).
4. Compose: take the row's opening skeleton and bend it with the column's device. Then verbalize ([BTA:13]) and intensify ([BTA:06]).

## Cross-axis quick key — which technique dominates each cell
| Awareness ↓ / Soph → | 1 First | 2 Second | 3 Claims done | 4 Mech. done | 5 Exhausted |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 Most Aware** | Price/offer | Better price | Offer + mech. tag | Offer + mech.+ | Offer + identity |
| **2 Product-Aware** | Name+claim | Bigger claim | Feature mechanism [BTA:10] | Elaborate mechanism | Identify [BTA:07] |
| **3 Solution-Aware** | Crystallize desire | Amplify desire | Desire via new mechanism | Mechanism embellish | Identity + desire |
| **4 Problem-Aware** | Name problem | Sharpen problem | Problem→new-mechanism cure | Mechanism-cure race | Identity + problem |
| **5 Unaware** | Image/emotion lead [BTA:07] | — (rare) | — | — | Pure identification |

Rule of thumb: as you move **right**, stop competing on the promise and shift to *how* it works, then to *who* the buyer is. As you move **down**, the headline must create more recognition before it can sell.

## Row 1 — Most Aware  (knows product, wants it, hasn't bought)
Skeleton: **[Product name] + [offer/price/reason to act now]**. Persuasion already done; the deal is the message. Sophistication barely matters — at most, add a one-word mechanism tag or identity cue to break ties.
- **EN:** "Notion Plus — 40% off through Sunday, keep every template you build." · Tie-break with identity: "The workspace 2M builders run their week on — now 40% off."
- **RU:** "Яндекс Плюс — годовая подписка за полцены до воскресенья, весь каталог и озвучка." · Идентичность-довесок: "Подписка, с которой смотрит вся семья — сейчас за полцены."
- **How not to:** re-explaining what the product does or piling on proof — it bores a ready buyer and buries the offer. Never lead with a mechanism here.

## Row 2 — Product-Aware  (knows product, not yet sold)
Skeleton: **[Product name] + [superiority claim]**; body elaborates the superiority. The seven ch-2 sub-tasks apply (reinforce desire, sharpen image, extend use, add proof, new mechanism, remove limitation, change image).
- **Soph 1–2 (claim race):** state / then out-bid the superiority claim.
  - EN: "Linear is the fastest issue tracker your team will ever use." → bigger: "The only tracker that stays instant at 10,000 open issues."
  - RU: "Kaiten — доска задач, которая не тормозит на 300 карточках." → сильнее: "Единственная доска, которая летает даже на 5000 задач."
- **Soph 3–4 (feature the mechanism [BTA:10]):** put *how* in the headline, restate the claim below.
  - EN: "Linear stays instant because it renders every change locally first — no server round-trip." (mechanism as the point of difference)
  - RU: "Kaiten не тормозит, потому что считает изменения на вашем устройстве, а не на сервере — вот почему."
- **Soph 5 (identify [BTA:07]):** drop claims, lead with who the user is.
  - EN: "Built for teams that ship on Fridays and mean it."
  - RU: "Для команд, которые релизят по пятницам — и не боятся этого."
- **How not to:** headlining a physical spec instead of the doing ([BTA:03]) — "Now with GraphQL API" instead of the outcome it buys.

## Row 3 — Solution-Aware  (wants the result, doesn't know your product)
Skeleton: **Crystallize the still-vague desire/solution in the headline, then attach your product.** Name the want sharply; prove it's achievable; show your product is the mechanism.
- **Soph 1–2 (bare / amplified desire):**
  - EN: "Fall asleep in 15 minutes — without grogginess the next day." → amplified: "Asleep in 15 minutes, sharp by 7 a.m. — every single night."
  - RU: "Засыпайте за 15 минут — и просыпайтесь без разбитости." → сильнее: "15 минут до сна и ясная голова к семи утра — каждый день."
- **Soph 3–4 (desire through a new mechanism):** the promise is disbelieved; lead with the fresh mechanism.
  - EN: "New: a cooling pad that pulls heat from your core — asleep in 15 minutes because your body thinks it's midnight in October."
  - RU: "Новинка: охлаждающая подушка, которая отводит тепло от тела — засыпаете за 15 минут, потому что тело думает, что уже глубокая осень."  <!-- privacy-ok: термин из книжного конспекта, не финансы владельца -->
- **Soph 5 (identity + desire):**
  - EN: "For people who've tried every sleep hack and quietly given up — one more thing, and it isn't a pill."
  - RU: "Для тех, кто перепробовал всё для сна и махнул рукой — есть ещё один способ, и это не таблетка."
- **How not to:** educating the desire into existence ([BTA:01]) — "Understanding your circadian rhythm" teaches instead of crystallizing an existing want.

## Row 4 — Problem-Aware  (feels the problem, hasn't linked it to a product)
Skeleton: **Name the problem (and/or its solution) + dramatize the need + present product as inevitable fix.** Ranges from the bare problem word to problem+solution+limitation-removed.
- **Soph 1–2 (name / sharpen the problem):**
  - EN: bare: "Cloud bill out of control?" → sharpened: "Your AWS bill doubled again — and no one on the team knows why."
  - RU: голо: "Счёт за облако растёт?" → острее: "Счёт за облако снова удвоился — и никто в команде не знает почему."
- **Soph 3–4 (problem → new-mechanism cure):**
  - EN: "Stop guessing at your cloud bill — a tracker that tags every dollar to the exact query that spent it."
  - RU: "Хватит гадать по счёту за облако — сервис, который привязывает каждый рубль к конкретному запросу, который его потратил."
- **Soph 5 (identity + problem):**
  - EN: "For the engineer who gets pinged every time finance opens the AWS console."
  - RU: "Для инженера, которому пишут каждый раз, когда финансисты открывают консоль облака."
- **How not to:** stating the solution before the need stings — a prospect who doesn't feel the problem skips the fix. Dramatize first.

## Row 5 — Completely Unaware  (no felt desire/need, or unspeakable — also exhausted markets)
Skeleton: **No price, no product name.** Enter through a mirrored identity, emotion, or a widely-accepted image; build recognition; only then bridge to desire and product. This equals sophistication stage 5 — both solved by **identification** ([BTA:07]).
- **Pattern A — mirror the reader's world:**
  - EN: "The Sunday-night feeling starts around 6 p.m., doesn't it?" (names an unspoken state before any product)
  - RU: "Тревога воскресного вечера накрывает где-то к шести, правда?" (называет непроговорённое состояние до продукта)
- **Pattern B — accepted image as a doorway to an unacceptable problem:**
  - EN: open on the admired identity ("You're the one people come to when things break") to lead into an unadmitted burnout, then the product.
  - RU: начать с признанной роли («Ты — тот, к кому идут, когда всё ломается»), чтобы подвести к непризнанному выгоранию, и только потом продукт.
- **Pattern C — resentment / unvoiced protest:**
  - EN: "Nobody tells you that 'unlimited PTO' means you take less."
  - RU: "Никто не говорит вслух, что «безлимитный отпуск» — это когда отдыхаешь меньше."
- **How not to:** opening with the product, its name, or a price — all mean nothing to an unaware market and waste the headline. No claim-stacking; lead with identity/emotion only.

## Composition checklist (any cell)
1. Row skeleton chosen from **awareness** → correct opening content.
2. Device chosen from **sophistication** → claim / mechanism / identity level.
3. Pick the dominant desire on the three dimensions ([BTA:04]); headline the doing, not the spec ([BTA:03]).
4. Verbalize ([BTA:13]): measure it, time it, question it, or tease the mechanism.
5. Lead body with belief-bridge ([BTA:08]) if the claim is a leap; intensify ([BTA:06]); prove mechanism ([BTA:10]); destroy alternatives ([BTA:11]); borrow trust via native format ([BTA:12]) — ethically, no deceptive impersonation.
